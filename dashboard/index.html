Key Operational Insights

1. Provider access: The average arrival-to-provider wait was approximately 70 minutes.

3. Staffing: Provider counts did not show a strong relationship with average wait time in this synthetic dataset.

Executive Summary & Recommendations

Key Findings

  • The ED recorded 1,000 patient visits with an average provider wait of 70.02 minutes.
  • 295 visits, or 29.50%, experienced a provider wait longer than 90 minutes.
  • Average ED length of stay was approximately 299.51 minutes, or about 5 hours.
  • Average wait times were relatively consistent across Day, Evening, and Night shifts.
  • Provider staffing levels from 2 to 5 showed minimal differences in average wait time.
  • Hourly analysis showed limited variation in average wait times across the 24-hour period.

Operational Recommendations

  • Monitor the percentage of patients waiting more than 90 minutes as a key performance indicator.
  • Evaluate patient flow and provider assignment processes rather than relying solely on staffing increases.
  • Continue monitoring wait times by shift, triage level, and arrival hour to identify emerging bottlenecks.
  • Use additional operational data in future analysis to determine which workflow factors most strongly influence delays.
